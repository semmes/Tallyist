From what’s on tap, to on tap.

Not just for the beer drinkers. The Today screen and the Home Screen widget both log a drink with a single tap. Everything else is optional and happens later.

A calendar you can read at a glance.

Days shade by amount. Weeks and months add up.

Say what it was, when you feel like it.

Beer, wine, spirit, cocktail. Size and strength if you want them. Or leave it as a drink, which is also fine.

{% include phone.html name="detail-sheet" alt="The drink sheet over the Today screen: Beer selected, a 12 oz can, strength set to 5% ABV, equal to about 1 standard drink, and a Log drink button." %}

Yesterday counts too.

Add a drink to any past day, change one, or remove one. The log is yours to keep accurate.

Your weeks, your weekdays, your weekends.

Charts of what you logged, side-by-side comparisons of your own days, and optional references from published US surveys, each named and dated in the app. The dashed line is your own average, not a target.

Session pacing

An optional card that shows how many drinks you have logged in the current sitting, when it started, and when the last one was. Off by default. It sets no limit and sends no notification.

Same app, same rules. No account is required. Tallyist asks for no sign-in, and has no permission to use the internet.

Everything else.

  • Appearance

    System, light, or dark.

  • Standard drink size

    United States, United Kingdom, or Australia. Past days recount when you change it.

  • Apple Health

    Optional. Saves your drinks to Health if you allow it, and shows drinks other apps recorded there.

  • iCloud sync

    Your log on your own devices, through your own iCloud.

  • Fill a stretch

    Press and drag across the calendar to fill several days at once.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Log by voice with Siri, or from Shortcuts and the Action button.

  • Share a month or year

    An image of the calendar and its totals, made on your device, sent only where you send it.

  • CSV export

    Your whole log as a spreadsheet file.

Yours, and only yours.

Apple Health is optional, and the app works without it.

No servers
