Keep the conversation going.

Hold down the plus to log the type, or a quick tap just logs the drink and you can say what it was later on in the app. Tap the number and it’ll hide your count.

{% include watch.html name="counter" eager=true alt="The watch counter: 3 drinks today, about 3 standard drinks, between a minus and a plus button, with the 1–2, 3–5, 6–9 and 10+ legend below." %} {% include watch.html name="type-picker" eager=true alt="The watch type picker: Beer, Wine, Spirit, Cocktail, and Other." %}

Today’s count, on the face or in the Smart Stack.

A complication shows the number you have logged today. Tap it to open the counter. In the Smart Stack, the card has its own plus.

{% include watch.html name="face-complication" alt="The Smart Stack on Apple Watch at 10:30. The Tallyist card shows 4 drinks today, a plus button, and four dots for the current session." %}

Sessions, as a row of dots.

One dot for each drink in the current session. Countable at a glance, and nothing more. Off until you turn it on.

{% include watch.html name="session-dots" alt="The watch counter at 3 drinks today, with three dots below it for the current sitting and the line 3 · 1m." %}

The watch shows what you already know and hides it from everyone else.

In the app, tap the count to hide it, and it hides by itself when you lower your wrist. On the watch face, it hides while the watch is locked, as it is once you take it off.

The watch app installs with the iPhone app.
