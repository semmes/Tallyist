Press

Tallyist is a free {% if state >= 2 %}app for iPhone and Apple Watch{% else %}iPhone app{% endif %} that counts what you drink and shows your own pattern: a calendar shaded by amount, weekly and monthly totals, and your own average. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no goals, and no advice.
